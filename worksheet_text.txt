💡(샘플) '대퇴사'는 직장을 그만두거나 직업을 바꾸는 근로자가 증가하는 추세를 말

또한 원격 근무를 하던 많은 사람들이 자율성을 포기하지 않으려 했습니다. 그 결
과 직장을 그만두고 다른 곳에서 더 나은 기회를 찾고 있습니다.
/g28D‘The Great Resignation’ refers to the growing trend of workers quiting or 
just changing careers, and there are several reasons why this is 
happening. First, many workers are feeling burned out after working 
through a year that had so many challenges. Additionally, the pandemic 
made them reflect on their lives and realize what they really want. So, that 
led them to make new decisions about their future. Furthermore, a number 
of people who worked remotely have been unwilling to give up their 
autonomy. As a result, they are quitting their jobs and seeking better 
opportunities elsewhere.
